📖 Quoridor Evaluation Weights Glossary

MyDist (my_distance_weight)
Priority on shortening the bot's own path to the goal row.
RivalDist (rival_distance_weight)
Priority on lengthening the opponent's path to their goal row.
WallRes (wall_reserve_weight)
Value assigned to saving unplaced walls for crucial endgame moments.
Centrality (centrality_weight)
Preference for positioning the pawn near center files for maximum agility.
JumpPenalty (jump_conceded_penalty)
Penalty for positioning adjacent to opponent, conceding a jump advantage.
Funneling (funneling_bonus)
Reward for wall placement creating chokepoints or long detours.
